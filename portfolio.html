03 / Research · NEPT 2025 · First author

Carbon footprint prediction & RL recommendations

ConForMiSt combines emissions prediction with reinforcement learning for recommendation optimization.

Machine learning · Reinforcement learning · Recommendation systems

The problem

Predict carbon emissions and use those predictions to inform recommendations for electrical appliances and transport vehicles.

My contribution

Designed a dual-phase framework that combines machine learning for carbon footprint prediction with reinforcement learning to generate recommendations.

Results

Achieved RMSE as low as 0.06 kgCO₂e and reduced emission variance by up to 10%.

ML emissions prediction → RL recommendations → Emission variance evaluation

Blood pressure prediction from PPG signals

Developed a deep learning model to predict blood pressure from photoplethysmography waveforms.

Deep learning · Physiological signals · Regression

The task

Model the relationship between PPG waveforms and blood pressure.

Drone detection with YOLOv5

YOLOv5 · Object detection · Model evaluation

The task

My contribution

06 / Research · IEEE NMITCON 2023 · Best paper

Microbial fuel-cell voltage prediction

Used a CNN-LSTM regressor to predict electricity generation from microbial fuel cells.

Time series · CNN-LSTM · Regression

The task

Predict microbial fuel-cell voltage output using pH, TSS, and COD time-series data.

My contribution

Reconstructed the three time series with a graph digitizer and developed a CNN-LSTM regressor.

Results

Achieved 0.04 V RMSE on test data. The associated IEEE NMITCON 2023 publication received the Best Research Paper Award.
