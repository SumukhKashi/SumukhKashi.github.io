Bio

I’m a master’s student in Computer Science at UMass Amherst, interested in natural language processing, large language models, and reinforcement learning for sequential decision-making.

My research spans English-to-Kannada neural machine translation, RL-based recommendation optimization, and applied machine learning. I have five peer-reviewed publications, including two first-author papers and a Best Paper Award at IEEE NMITCON.

Previously, I spent two years at Honeywell Aerospace, where I built an LLM-based retrieval system for engineering documentation and worked on safety-critical avionics software. My experience also includes deep learning for physiological signals at iMedrix Systems and a US patent filing.

I’m seeking Summer 2027 research internships in NLP/LLM systems.
